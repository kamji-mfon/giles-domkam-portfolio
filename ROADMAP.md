# Evidence roadmap

This is an evidence roadmap, not a delivery schedule or a claim that a pilot has been approved. Dates and owners for future work have not been assigned.

| Stage | Success criterion | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Public synthetic examples | A reader can run the documented examples and understand their limits | Available; see [checks](technical/review-process.md) |
| Private simulator evidence | A repaired simulator and evidence gate fail reliably on failed assertions and produce reproducible, reviewed results | Blocked by reconstruction gaps; see [status](technical/observation-evidence.md#simulator-status) |
| Authorized physical observation | Documented scope, data mapping, and safe observation of real equipment, with failures recorded | Not demonstrated |
| Timing and recovery | Agreed requirements and measurements for the intended hardware, clock, and network | Not demonstrated |
| Independent review | A qualified reviewer can inspect the claim, evidence, methodology, and limits | Not demonstrated |

Progression depends on owner authorization, appropriate safety review, availability of equipment and reference data, and an agreed evidence scope. A completed simulation does not satisfy physical validation.
